# App, auth and transaction pages are kept out of the index by
# <meta name="robots" content="noindex"> ON THE PAGES THEMSELVES, not by
# Disallow. Disallow does not deindex: a blocked page that has inbound
# links still gets indexed, titleless, and because Google may not fetch
# it, it never sees the noindex that would remove it. That is what put
# these URLs into "Indexed, though blocked by robots.txt". Letting the
# crawler in is what takes them back out, so do not re-add Disallow
# lines for:
#   /dashboard.html  /qbank.html  /qbank-diag.html  /practice-exam.html
#   /success.html    /login.html  /signup.html

# Paid study chapters. These are HTML fragments with no <head>, loaded
# into the dashboard by JS, so they cannot carry a noindex of their own.
# Blocking is the only control available for them.
